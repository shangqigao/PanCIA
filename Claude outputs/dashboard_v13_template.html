Tumour size vs stage: BiomedParse (2D) vs VoxTell (3D)

TCGA-TCIA public cohort · 15 cancer types · 4,968 CT/MR series from 996 patients · RECIST 1.1 longest diameter on axial slices · Spearman ρ against AJCC T stage and overall stage
v13 · 16 Sep 2026public data only

Conclusion

VoxTell's RECIST longest diameter tracks pathological T stage better than BiomedParse's in 12 of 13 cancer types with T-stage data, and is significantly associated with T stage in 9 of them (BRCA, CESC, COAD, ESCA, KICH, KIRC, LIHC, LUSC, STAD) versus 4 for BiomedParse (CESC, KIRC, LIHC, STAD). The same pattern holds for tumour volume (VT significant in 9, BP in 7). BiomedParse leads only in cervical cancer (CESC), where 2D slice-wise MR segmentation captures FIGO/T extent well, and in endometrial cancer (UCEC) at overall stage. Neither model correlates with stage in bladder (BLCA), lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD) or prostate (PRAD), whose T stage is defined by depth of invasion or extraprostatic extension rather than size — an expected null. Correlations are modest overall (|ρ| mostly 0.1–0.4): stage is a coarse ordinal target and 24% of VoxTell outputs contain no RECIST-measurable lesion.

How the numbers were produced

Clinical stage. GDC clinical.tsv per project. One row per patient taken entirely from the primary diagnosis record (<case>_diagnosis, classification "primary"); synchronous/prior primaries and metastases are ignored. T = ajcc_pathologic_t → 1–4 (Tis/T0/TX excluded). Overall = ajcc_pathologic_stage, or figo_stage for CESC/OV/UCEC → I–IV (substages collapsed).
Axial plane. The superior–inferior axis is identified from the NIfTI affine for every scan; measurements are made on true anatomical axial slices even for the 18% of series acquired sagittally or coronally (mostly breast and cervix MR). Slice thickness = spacing along S–I.
RECIST 1.1 longest diameter. Masks are split into 3D connected components (6-connectivity). For each component the longest edge-to-edge caliper on any single axial slice is computed (convex hull of boundary-pixel corners). A component is measurable if LD ≥ 10 mm, or ≥ 2 × slice thickness when thickness > 5 mm. The index lesion is the largest measurable component; series with none score 0 mm. Identical rule for both models.
Volume. Total mask volume = voxel count × |det(affine)| in mm³ (all components, including VoxTell fragments). Index-lesion volume is also stored in the per-series file.
Statistics. Spearman rank correlation of each measure with ordinal stage, per project, over all series. Patients contribute a mean of 5 series (67% of multi-series patients: same-day sequences), so p-values are optimistic; ρ is unaffected. * p<0.05, ** p<0.01, *** p<0.001.
Stage type. size T stage defined mainly by tumour dimension (BRCA, KICH, KIRC, KIRP, LIHC, LUAD, LUSC) · invasion by depth of invasion / extension (BLCA, CESC, COAD, ESCA, PRAD, STAD) · FIGO overall stage from FIGO (CESC, OV, UCEC). OV and UCEC have no AJCC T in TCGA; PRAD has no overall stage.

Reading the results

  • Size-staged cancers behave as expected. In KIRC (n=1,124 series), LIHC (855) and BRCA (634) both models' diameters and volumes rise with T stage; VoxTell is consistently stronger (KIRC LD ρ +0.22 vs +0.13; BRCA LD +0.16*** vs +0.03 ns). KICH (18 series, 6 patients) shows the largest ρ (+0.62/+0.70) but is too small to weight heavily.
  • VoxTell also captures invasion-staged tumours where BP does not: oesophageal (ESCA LD +0.39** vs −0.15), gastric (STAD +0.32*** vs +0.26**) and colon (COAD +0.56** vs +0.39 ns), i.e. 3D context helps where the tumour is a wall thickening rather than a mass.
  • BiomedParse wins in gynaecological MR. CESC T stage (LD +0.24*** vs +0.15**; volume +0.42*** vs +0.21***) and UCEC FIGO stage (LD +0.21*** vs +0.09 ns). These cohorts are 42%/10% sagittal or coronal MR with thin axial reformats, where 2D slice-wise detection is at its strongest and VoxTell fragments most (median 17 components in CESC).
  • Expected nulls. BLCA (depth of invasion; BP diameter even weakly negative, −0.14**), LUAD (n=91) and PRAD (extraprostatic extension; 14 patients) show no association for either model. Overall stage adds little beyond T except in KIRC/LIHC, and is flat in BRCA, STAD, OV and COAD where nodal or peritoneal disease drives stage.
  • Caveats. Multiple series per patient inflate n; small projects (COAD, ESCA, KICH, KIRP, PRAD: 6–19 patients) are exploratory; 24% of VoxTell series have no measurable lesion and score 0 mm, which is counted (not excluded) as a segmentation failure; total volume includes VoxTell noise fragments (index-lesion volume, in the CSV, gives near-identical ρ).

Reproducibility

  • Per-series measurements · ~/mnt/clinical/recist_measures_v3.csv (9,936 rows: series × model; components, measurable components, RECIST LD and slice index, index-lesion volume, total and largest-component volume, S–I axis, acquisition plane, slice thickness).
  • Per-patient clinical (primary diagnosis) · tcga_clinical_primary.csv (6,981 cases; raw T/stage/FIGO strings, encoded integers, selection rule, number of diagnoses).
  • Analysis table · seg_stage_analysis_v3.csv (4,968 series joined to patient stage, study date, modality) and correlations · stage_correlations_v3.csv.
  • Scripts · ~/mnt/clinical/scripts/recist_v3.py (measurements, resumable) and build_stage_analysis_v3.py (clinical extraction, join, Spearman). Masks: ~/mnt/TCGA_Seg/{BiomedParse,VoxTell}; clinical: GDC exports of 2026-09-15; series metadata: TCIA TCGA_pathology_has_radiology.csv.
  • Changes from v12 (audit of 16 Sep 2026). (1) Clinical merge previously mixed fields across diagnosis records — 24 imaged patients changed stage; BLCA overall-stage VT ρ (+0.11*) is no longer significant, LUSC T-stage VT volume rose to +0.31***. (2) Through-plane axis previously assumed to be array axis 2 — 242 scans (mostly sagittal breast MR) were measured in the wrong plane. (3) Diameter was the in-plane bounding-box side of the whole mask; now RECIST single-slice longest diameter per component, so COAD BP diameter dropped from +0.61** to +0.39 ns and ESCA VT rose from +0.28* to +0.39**. Volumes and Spearman code verified unchanged.
